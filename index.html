ENKO is a full-cycle music company from Ukraine. Distribution, publishing, marketing.

Ukraine’s largest independent music company, founded in Kyiv in 2020, with offices in Kyiv and Warsaw. We work with 170 artists, distribute through Universal Music Group, publish with Sony Music Publishing and Kobalt, and run marketing with our partner LYRA. Our artists won Eurovision in 2022 and placed top-3 in 2024.

    Distribution

    Through our Universal Music Group partnership: all major platforms, markets worldwide, releases pitched to editors properly. A personal distribution manager who knows your record cold, and royalty reporting with every stream itemized.

    Paid campaigns on Facebook, TikTok and YouTube, a promo plan for every release, and influencer campaigns end to end. Run with our partners at LYRA. It’s the reason the numbers on this page look the way they do.
